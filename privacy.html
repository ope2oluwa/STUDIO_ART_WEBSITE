Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5/9/26

1. Information We Collect

When you make a purchase, we collect your name, email address, and phone number solely for processing your order and communicating about your artwork.

2. Payment Information

All payments are processed securely through Paystack. We do not store or have access to your credit card details.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used exclusively for:

  • Processing and shipping your order
  • Sending order confirmations and updates
  • Responding to your inquiries

4. Data Storage

Order data is stored securely on Firebase servers. We retain order information for record-keeping purposes.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Paystack — Payment processing
  • Firebase — Data storage
  • Telegram — Customer communication

6. Contact

For privacy concerns, contact us at studio@elenavoss.com or via Telegram.
